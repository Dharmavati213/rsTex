# The accepted differences from the oracle: run.py reads this.  A line is the case, then a reason a reader can check.  Each reason
# below was checked: docs/PORTING.md section 15, "Known divergences", has the two classes and what follows from them.
#
# The clamp: a dimension or number at or above 2^31 in a node's word, or in an eqtb entry, on the oracle, whose integer has 64 bits.
mlist/limits_wide clamp: \fontdimen8\tenex=5000pt makes the clearance of a \displaystyle{a\atop b} 7*5000pt (section 921), so section 923 makes a kern of 35000pt, past 2^31sp.  The oracle shows \kern35000.0 and here the node holds 2^31-1sp, \kern32767.99998.  It is the case's only difference, and no later line depends on the kern.
pack/limits clamp: \hbox{\k\k\k} is 3*16383.99998pt.  The oracle's \wd1 holds that, so 0.5\wd1 is past \maxdimen and "Dimension too large"; here \wd1 holds 2^31-1sp, whose half is just \maxdimen.  It is the case's only difference.
scan/dimen_units clamp: \hbox{\hskip 16383pt\hskip 16383pt\hskip 16383pt} is 49149pt on the oracle and 2^31-1sp here, so \showbox0 prints x32767.99998 for x49149.0, .5\wd0 has no "Dimension too large", .25\wd0 is 8191.99998pt for 12287.25pt and .1\wd0 is 3276.99998pt for 4915.19998pt.  These are the case's only differences.
#
# The banner: the version string in the job id.  With " (TeX Live 2026/Arch Linux)" in VERSION_STRING both cases agree: checked.
pdfout/resname banner: \pdfuniqueresname's prefix is the CRC of the job id (utils.c setjobid and getresnameprefix), and the job id holds the version string, " (rstex)" here, so /F1QUkwD0 here is /F1i47QM4 on the oracle.  The PDF is otherwise the same.
pdfship/uniqueres banner: as pdfout/resname: /F1olhFP1 here is /F1BYPlB1 on the oracle, from the CRC of a job id with another version string in it.
#
# Old words: what a node's word held before get_node gave it out.  On both sides it is uninitialised memory, read by the program.
control/interword_head_char old words: a space first in its list reads the head's cell as a char node, section 705.  With no token between a freed char node and that head, the cell holds the char node on the oracle too, and the glue takes its \stbscode, which here is left alone, since any token before it would have made it a token's word on the oracle and here nothing records that.
pack/expansion_stale_null old words: section 840 leaves the glue fields of a line box whose font_expand_ratio rounds to 0, and over old noads glue_sign and glue_order are an lh of null, which is 0 here and -268435455 on the oracle, whose low halves read as sign -4096 and order fil.  So the oracle shows a glue set, 0.0fil or a NaN, and this shows none.  The PDF is the same.
pdfship/dest_stale_pointer old words: as pdfship/dest_stale_null, and the words here are pointers into variable-size memory, one more here because mem_bot is 1 for its 0.  So 16 of 40 /XYZ differ, by 0.0001 or 0.0002 under the matrix of 7.  The log is the same.
pdfship/dest_stale_null old words: under a matrix do_dest reads the width, height and depth of an xyz destination, which section 1565 never set, and over a freed empty box one of those words is an old list_ptr, null, which is 0 here and -268435455 on the oracle: some /XYZ have y 4096pt higher there.  docs/PORTING.md section 15; pdfship/dest_stale_words, with other old words, agrees.
#
# Memory never set, on the oracle only.
image/cli:stack_end_at_a_file uninitialised: section 350 says incr(in_open) and then push_input, whose overflow shows the context with index one below in_open, so section 335 prints line_stack[index+1], which nothing has set: malloc's memory on the oracle, l.7310012246412718707 on one run, and 0 here.
#
# Capacity: token lists are not in mem here, and there is no pool file.  docs/PORTING.md section 15, "Where main memory fills" and "Numbers in overflow messages".
toks/fills_memory capacity: the oracle's 5000000 words fill up with token lists and it stops, where they are not in mem here and the job ends.  With extra_mem_top=5000000 the oracle ends too, and the two agree: toks/fills_memory_room.
files/pool_full capacity: both stop at the same name, and "pool size=" is pool_size less what the format holds, which is 30252 more here, as the oracle's pool file is not.  The only line that differs.
