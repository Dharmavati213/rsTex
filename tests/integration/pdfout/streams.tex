% stream.  Data ending just before and just after the 16384 bytes of the buffer, so pdf_flush falls inside the stream
% and at its very end.  A /Length field that pdf_flush cuts in two: the padding objects put the buffer's end in the middle
% of it for one of the sizes tried.  Lengths of several digits overwrite only their own places, the rest stays blank.
